# 🖨️ Xero-kit – Smart Xerox & Printing Service

### Upload. Print. Deliver. 📄🚚

Xero-kit is an on-demand Xerox and printing service that simplifies document printing for students, professionals, and businesses. Users can upload documents online, preview files, check printing costs, and request doorstep delivery without visiting a printing shop.

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Traditional Xerox and printing services require customers to visit shops, wait in queues, and spend time collecting printed documents. Xero-kit aims to make printing faster, more convenient, affordable, and privacy-focused.

## ✨ Key Features

- 📄 Online document upload
- 👀 Document preview before printing
- 💰 Transparent printing prices
- 📑 Accurate page ordering
- 🔒 Privacy-focused document handling
- 🚚 Doorstep delivery
- ⚡ Convenient and time-saving service

## 🎯 Target Audience

- College and school students
- Teachers and educational institutions
- Working professionals
- Small businesses
- Anyone who needs printing services

## 💡 Our Solution

Xero-kit connects customers with printing services through a convenient online platform. Customers can submit documents, review printing requirements, understand the cost, and arrange delivery to save time and effort.
